# Business Model

Zestify operates on a highly scalable, high-margin B2C SaaS model, with a planned expansion into B2B.

## 1. Freemium B2C Model (Consumer)

### The "Basic" Tier: Free
**Features**:
- Link up to 2 financial institutions via Plaid.
- Basic Net Worth tracking dashboard.
- Monthly Zest-Score calculation.
**Purpose**: Customer Acquisition. The free tier acts as a lead generator, hooking users on the UI and the concept of the Zest-Score without incurring massive LLM API costs.

### The "Pro" Tier: $14.99 / month (or $120 / year)
**Features**:
- Unlimited Plaid institution connections.
- Unlimited queries to the **ZUST AI Copilot**.
- Real-time Portfolio Diversification analysis.
- Advanced What-If Simulators and Goal Planning.
**Unit Economics**:
- Expected Plaid API cost per user: ~$0.30 / month.
- Expected LLM API cost per user: ~$1.50 / month.
- Gross Margin: ~85%.

## 2. Expansion Model (B2B SaaS)

### Zestify for Advisors: $199 / month / seat
Independent Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) are desperate for modern software to retain younger clients.
**Features**:
- White-labeled dashboard for their clients.
- "God Mode" view of all client Zest-Scores to identify who needs intervention or upsells.
- AI-generated quarterly portfolio reviews.

## 3. Go-To-Market Strategy
1. **Organic Growth via "Zest-Score" Sharing**: Similar to how people share Spotify Wrapped or Wordle scores, users can share a visually appealing graphic of their Zest-Score (without revealing absolute net worth dollars) on Twitter/LinkedIn, driving viral acquisition.
2. **Finfluencer Partnerships**: Sponsor personal finance creators on YouTube and TikTok, providing them with custom referral codes.
